Hospital Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze patient admissions, billing, revenue, healthcare costs, payment sources, and patient satisfaction.

Dashboard Preview

![Hospital Analytics Dashboard](Screenshot-%20Hospital%20Dashboard.png)

Business Objective

- Analyze hospital patient admissions and monthly trends
- Monitor billing and revenue performance
- Understand healthcare cost distribution
- Analyze patient satisfaction levels
- Identify trends to support data-driven decision making

Key Performance Indicators

- Total Patients: 4,973
- Total Billing Amount: 4M
- Total Room Charges: 1.05608M
- Average Satisfaction Score: 3.84

Key Analysis

- Year-on-Year Monthly Admissions: Compares patient admissions across 2024 and 2025
- Patient Satisfaction: Analyzes patients across satisfaction scores from 1 to 5
- Payment Source Distribution: Shows the distribution of hospital payments
- Cost Distribution: Analyzes treatment, room, and medication costs
- Monthly Revenue Trend: Tracks revenue performance throughout the year

Key Insights

- Patient admissions show noticeable month-to-month fluctuations
- Higher patient volumes are observed at satisfaction scores of 1 and 2
- Treatment cost represents the largest share of analyzed hospital costs
- Monthly revenue reaches its highest level around April before declining
- One payment source contributes approximately 52.59% of total payments
